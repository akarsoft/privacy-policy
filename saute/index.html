Saute — merge, cook, chase the combo

Saute is a merge puzzle game for iPhone. Drag two matching ingredients together to level them up, fill the guests' orders, and unlock new cities as you go. The twist: merging fast is what pays.

  • 🔥 Combos. Merge again within 3.5 seconds and your gold multiplier climbs, up to ×4.
  • 👨‍🍳 Chef Mode. Reach combo 8 and it opens for 9 seconds: double gold, unlimited generators.
  • ⚡️ Triple merge. Three matching pieces side by side skip two tiers at once.
  • 🚫 No energy timers. Generators recharge on their own — the board is the only limit.
  • 🌍 Five ingredient chains, four cities, and the whole interface in 39 languages.
  • 🔒 No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no network access — privacy policy.

FAQ & Support

How do I merge?
Drag one piece on top of another identical piece. While dragging, a green tile means they will merge; a white one means they will swap places, so the board never jams.
What is the number under a generator?
Its charge. Tap the generator to produce a new ingredient; the charge refills by itself over time, even while the game is closed. There is no energy to wait for.
How do I deliver an order?
When an order card turns green you already have what the guest wants. Tap the card and the ingredients go out.
My board is full. What now?
Deliver an order, or press and hold any piece to sell it for gold. Generators cannot be sold.
Is my progress saved?
Yes, on your device. There is no account and nothing is uploaded anywhere.
How do I see the tutorial again?
Open the shop (cart icon) and choose "Replay the tutorial".
