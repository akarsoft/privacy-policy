Privacy Policy — Saute

Last updated: 20 August 2026

Short version

Saute collects nothing. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, and the app makes no network requests at all.

What the app stores

Your game progress — the board, your gold, level, orders and settings — is written to a single file inside the app's own container on your device. It never leaves your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes it.

What the app does not do

  • No personal information is requested, collected or stored.
  • No identifiers are collected, and nothing is used for tracking across apps or websites.
  • No third-party SDKs for analytics, attribution or advertising are included.
  • No data is transmitted to the developer or to anyone else.
  • There are no in-app purchases and no payment information is handled.

Children

Saute is suitable for all ages. Because no data is collected from anyone, no data is collected from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
