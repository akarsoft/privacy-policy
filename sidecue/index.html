FAQ & Support

Does it work with Zoom / Meet / Teams / OBS?
Yes. Full-screen sharing and recordings need no setup. For single-window sharing on Zoom 6.17+, keep "Advanced capture with window filtering" enabled in Zoom's settings (it's the default).
Can the other side ever see my notes?
No. The window is excluded at the compositor level, before any sharing app can access its pixels. The built-in demo lets you verify this with your own eyes on first launch.
Why does SideCue ask for Screen Recording permission?
Only for the optional onboarding demo, where it captures your own screen once to show you that your notes are absent from captures. You can skip it.
