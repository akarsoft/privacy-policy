Nefes — calm breathing, one click away

Nefes ("breath" in Turkish) is a minimal breathing exercise app for your Mac. A softly glowing circle guides your inhales and exhales — in a beautiful window or right from your menu bar.

  • 🫁 Four proven techniques: Box breathing (4-4-4-4), 4-7-8, Relaxed (4-6), Coherent (5-5)
  • 🖥 Lives in your menu bar — one click to a quick session between meetings
  • 🌍 Localized into 34 languages
  • 🔒 No accounts, no analytics, no network access — privacy policy
  • 💰 One-time purchase, no subscription

FAQ & Support

How do I start a session?
Pick a technique, press Start (or the space bar). Follow the circle: it grows as you breathe in, shrinks as you breathe out.
How do I quit the app?
Nefes stays in the menu bar when you close its window. Click the wind icon in the menu bar and choose "Quit Nefes".
Which macOS versions are supported?
macOS 13 Ventura and later.
