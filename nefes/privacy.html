Nefes — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 17, 2026

The short version

Nefes does not collect, store, or transmit any data. Period.

Details

  • No data collection. Nefes has no analytics, no tracking, no accounts, and no advertising.
  • No network access. The app runs entirely on your Mac and never connects to the internet.
  • No personal information. We never see your name, email, device information, or anything else about you.
  • App Store purchases are processed by Apple. We receive only anonymous, aggregated sales statistics from Apple.
